- User-visible mechanism novelty: minimum 7.
- Protocol-composition novelty: minimum 7.

## Weighted hackathon dimensions

- Product usefulness and target-user clarity: 30%.
- Quality and depth of Flare integration: 30%.
- Technical execution and demo quality: 20%.
- New work completed during the program: 10%.
- Distribution, traction, and continuation potential: 10%.

For each idea: six hard scores with reasons; five weighted scores with reasons; weighted total; prior-art collision analysis; exact-interface status; both removal tests; joined-proof assessment; failed-claim list; final rank.
