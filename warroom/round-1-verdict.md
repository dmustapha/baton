# Round 1 Verdict — Rejected Finalist Set

Date: 2026-08-13
Decision owner: Dami
Status: Both finalists rejected; no winner selected

## Mandate Zero

Rejected as a finalist. Its Flare composition is coherent, but its user-visible mechanism reduces to familiar revocable, expiring, or one-cycle authorization. Broad primary-source prior art includes temporary approvals, expiring approvals, smart-account spending limits, and session-key delegation. A novel infrastructure composition is not enough when the product reads as “turn off auto-debit, then permit one bounded debit.”

## SplitLock

Rejected as a finalist. Its private policy-intersection mechanic is more distinctive, but the exact interoperable action is not presently supported by a verified public builder path. STP.13 describes PMWs on Songbird, while no public callable PMW interface or example was found for the proposed build and the Coston2 assumption failed.

## Binding correction for Round 2

Round 2 must preserve blind, generative corpus mining and add two mandatory post-freeze hard gates:

1. A Global Mechanism Prior-Art Gate covering standards, wallet and smart-account modules, established DeFi primitives, and shipped products, using primary sources wherever possible.
2. An Exact Callable-Interface Gate requiring the official function or interface, supported network, accessible builder path, controlled state transition, and judge-visible receipt for every claimed asset action.

Mechanism novelty, protocol-composition novelty, demo surprise, and exact-action feasibility must be scored separately. A concept fails if its plain-language description collapses to a familiar mechanism with Flare names substituted, unless a genuinely new load-bearing state transition or user outcome survives the substitution.

Forge and implementation remain prohibited. Round 2 stops at a fresh Dami finalist-selection checkpoint.
