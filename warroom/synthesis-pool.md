# Synthesized Idea Pool — Flare Summer Signal

Created only after the 20-idea raw pool was durably frozen. Differentiation and named collision analysis begin here.

## S1 — Late Route (merged with TagBack)

- **Center:** precommitted private recovery envelope for an XRP payment arriving after its direct-mint session expired; quarantine the resulting FXRP, then deliver or refund exactly once.
- **Imported mechanics:** TagBack's two-party claim fragments and claim nullifier.
- **Differentiation:** post-payment exception custody, not better minting; the irreversible source payment exists, the browser intent is dead, and only the committed private recovery envelope can release quarantined value.
- **Catalog:** CLEAR; closed-loop diagnosis and one-use nullification are remixed into an interrupted FAssets lifecycle.
- **Raw sources:** A1 + E1.

## S2 — Repair Receipt (merged with Redemption Rewind)

- **Center:** deterministic partial-redemption capsule; FCC privately compares bounded repair branches, while fresh FDC evidence permits one specific corrective action and produces a replay bundle.
- **Imported mechanics:** Redemption Rewind's counterfactual branch simulator and smallest-safe-action rule.
- **Differentiation:** begins with one failed redemption and must end with restored user value; diagnosis, monitoring, or risk scoring alone do not qualify.
- **Catalog:** CATALOG-ADJACENT; combines honest evidence labels and closed-loop diagnosis, but applies them to a Flare lifecycle repair.
- **Raw sources:** B1 + D4; FixBond is an optional later business model, not part of the core.

## S3 — SplitLock

- **Center:** FCC computes the safe intersection of two concurrently valid private policy versions and either authorizes one bounded PMW XRP payment or signs abstention.
- **Differentiation:** resolves a real split-brain signer/policy epoch; it is not generic treasury policy or payroll.
- **Catalog:** CLEAR; multi-party high-stakes resolution and local safety boundaries come from different families and produce a new external-asset execution rule.
- **Raw sources:** D3; A3/B3 machine-continuity mechanics are reduced to the single split-brain case.

## S4 — Mandate Zero

- **Center:** a Smart Account asset mandate deterministically decays to zero after one cycle; FCC must privately re-derive the only next-cycle cap before another FXRP action can execute.
- **Differentiation:** the demo begins at zero authority, not at yield allocation; forced expiration and confidential recreation are the product.
- **Catalog:** CLEAR; forced memory scarcity is transformed into forced financial re-authorization rather than copied at the surface.
- **Raw source:** D2.

## S5 — Cipher Refund Relay

- **Center:** an FDC-proven XRP payment plus a private claim produces one nullified, least-authority PMW refund and external reconciliation receipt.
- **Differentiation:** outbound external XRP remedy rather than checkout, escrow, or FXRP reimbursement.
- **Catalog:** CATALOG-ADJACENT; pre/post guardian and private decision patterns are familiar, but the causal PMW refund is distinct.
- **Raw source:** C3.

## S6 — Veiled Exit

- **Center:** private epoch allocation of a fixed, externally evidenced FXRP redemption-capacity event, with inclusion proofs and deterministic refunds.
- **Differentiation:** not trading, order routing, or exit-depth monitoring; the sole output is a fair allocation of one scarce redemption window.
- **Catalog:** CATALOG-ADJACENT; private batch settlement plus a first-session countdown are recognizable winning patterns.
- **Raw source:** E3; C2/D1 private-exit mechanics are deduplicated into this stronger multi-user allocation.

## S7 — FixBond

- **Center:** a preselected maintainer earns an FXRP bond only when a confidential deterministic replay proves a patch and the same flow restores a failed FAsset state.
- **Differentiation:** payment is secondary to verified asset repair; it is not a task marketplace or report-severity bounty.
- **Catalog:** CATALOG-ADJACENT; stake-backed claim plus live performance receipt.
- **Raw sources:** E2 + C4 inclusion/nullifier safeguard.

## S8 — Void Receipt

- **Center:** private inclusion accumulator plus a concrete FDC-resolvable event yields either an FXRP payout root or mandatory refund root, with omission challenge.
- **Differentiation:** failure-complete settlement and inclusion proof, not a generalized prediction market.
- **Catalog:** CATALOG-ADJACENT; private outcome settlement and countdown/reveal are established primitives.
- **Raw source:** E4.
