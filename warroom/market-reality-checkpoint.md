## Structural finding

Four requirements currently conflict:

1. The product must address a broad, recognizable, evidenced market.
2. Both FXRP/Interoperable Assets and FCC/FCE must be independently load-bearing.
3. Every transition must use a current public interface and existing actor authority.
4. The user-visible mechanism must be genuinely novel, not a familiar guard, policy engine, escrow, recovery, approval, or scoring product.

Current evidence shows:

- **Real FXRP market:** holders entering XRPFi, FAssets agents, Smart Account executors, and wallet/vault integrations.
- **Real private data:** holder preferences, agent keys/policy, executor credentials, exchange mappings, and business payment context.
- **Missing join:** broad users do not yet show demand for attested confidential computation, while FCC/FCE cannot control incumbent wallets, agent XRP signers, exchange ledgers, custodians, or external services without their explicit integration.
- **Native substitution:** FAssets and Smart Accounts already provide assignment, default compensation, replay/nonce binding, failed-mint recovery, fee replacement, and permissionless execution.
- **Novelty collapse:** the operable overlays reduce to established transaction guards, private policy engines, stop-losses, escrow, recovery, idempotency, or maker-checker controls.

## Decision options

### Option A — Keep every constraint

Conclude that no defensible project is currently evidenced. Do not submit an invented product.

### Option B — Target one track deeply

Run a fresh market-first Warroom for either:

- Interoperable Asset Products: broad, real FXRP product without forced FCC; or
- Confidential Compute Apps: a real confidential workflow without forced FXRP.

This changes the user's earlier `multi-track required` contract and requires explicit approval.

### Option C — Obtain a real pilot/integration

Secure one cooperating FAssets agent, Smart Account executor/wallet, vault, XRP-funded business, exchange sandbox, or custodian account. Its private workflow and authority could make FCC causally necessary. This requires external access and cannot be assumed.

## Recommendation

Choose **Option B** if the priority is a strong, generally acceptable hackathon product before the deadline. Choose **Option C** only if a real cooperating operator is immediately available. Do not run another unconstrained batch over the same evidence; it would repeat the same structural failures or manufacture a market.
