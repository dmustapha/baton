## Generator-Safe Creative Adaptation Deck

### [PRIMARY] Verifiable private decision

- **Sources:** TEE-sealed model ownership, TEE-signed treasuries, private AI with public proof, private payments, FHE voting/transfers/governance/trading.
- **Why it won:** judges could see a public consequence without receiving the private inputs.
- **Adaptation axes:** decision subject, minimum disclosure, stake, attestation, failure state, and who can challenge the result.

### [PRIMARY] External fact as state-transition trigger

- **Sources:** x402 payment security, verified knowledge publishing, oracle-backed position diagnosis, off-grid settlement, and cross-system payment products.
- **Why it won:** an offchain or cross-chain fact caused a concrete, auditable outcome.
- **Adaptation axes:** presence versus absence, time window, source chain, disputed evidence, and recovery action.

### [PRIMARY] Policy-controlled autonomous execution

- **Sources:** transaction guardians, reusable agent authorization, AI-managed treasuries, yield rotation, safety firewalls, and autonomous finance agents.
- **Why it won:** autonomy was bounded by a visible policy and produced real transactions.
- **Adaptation axes:** policy author, signer custody, simulation, limits, abstention, rotation, and emergency recovery.

### [PRIMARY] Privacy-preserving value flow

- **Sources:** hidden token transfers, private swaps, anonymous governance, private expense splitting, institutional private payments, TEE-verified services.
- **Why it won:** privacy changed who could safely transact or how execution occurred; it was not a UI feature.
- **Adaptation axes:** hidden field, timing, selective disclosure, settlement asset, recipient class, and audit receipt.

### [PRIMARY] Novel DeFi state machine

- **Sources:** agent-managed portfolios, structured prediction products, yield tokenization, BTC-backed assets, composable derivatives, AI-rebalanced yield, and chain-native credit.
- **Why it won:** one economic mechanic was explored deeply and visibly moved value.
- **Adaptation axes:** asset lifecycle, trigger, failure mode, collateral, claim priority, and exit/recovery rule.

### [PRIMARY] Honest evidence labels

## Winning Patterns

1. One novel mechanic, deeply explored.
2. Demo is the product: a visible action, not an architecture diagram.
3. Chain-native: remove the chain primitive and the outcome fails.
4. Live and value-producing beats hypothetical capability.
5. Autonomous decisions need onchain accountability.
6. Privacy or security must change the core mechanic.

## Anti-Patterns

1. Infrastructure that only enables instead of performing a valuable outcome.
2. Broad integration count with shallow dependence.
3. Responsible engineering presented as the idea.
4. Bottom-up construction without a 90-second proof path.
5. Testnet token economics presented as innovation.

## Track Strategy Contract

Every raw idea must contain both of these distinct, independently necessary primitives:

- **Interoperable Asset Products:** a real FAssets/FXRP lifecycle, Smart Account authorization, PMW external-asset action, or FDC-verified asset transition.
- **Confidential Compute Apps:** an FCC/FCE operation with explicit private inputs, disclosure boundary, machine/attestation status, signed result, and contract verification.

Removal test each primitive separately. Then join them in one path: user trigger → interoperable asset action/state → necessary confidential operation → verified result → asset consequence → judge-visible receipt.
