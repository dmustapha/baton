# Catalog and Prior-Project Analysis — Flare Summer Signal

## Scope and Method

This is the first catalog-aware pass after all 20 raw ideas were durably recorded. It compares the complete raw pool against the full primitives sheet, the gate-only shipped-project appendix, and persisted sibling `WINNER-BRIEF`/`PRD` directions. It does not score concepts and does not start finalist deliberation.

Collision labels used below:

- **Convergence:** adapts a catalog mechanism family but changes the user, state machine, and economic consequence.
- **Adjacency:** shares a domain or one load-bearing primitive with a prior project, but not the same product loop.
- **Overlap:** repeats the prior product category and most of its causal loop; it should not advance unless a genuinely new load-bearing primitive survives synthesis.
- **In-flight conflict:** materially duplicates an active sibling direction; under the gate rule it should not advance as a standalone concept regardless of chain.

## Portfolio Collision Baseline

The highest-risk prior directions are:

- **Veil, 9ncore, and in-flight PrivLend:** confidential lending/credit positions.
- **GhostFund:** private yield vaults and confidential allocation/exit policy.
- **ShadowDesk+ and Axon:** confidential trading and execution surfaces.
- **Backstop, AgentTreasury, and in-flight EdgeLedger:** bounded transaction authorization, signer separation, policy gating, abstention, and post-action reconciliation.
- **Verdikt / Verdikt-arc and solv-001:** adjudication/settlement and paid work by an agent.
- **AgentMesh, 0g-sentinel, Omnispect-X, and AgentAuditor:** security analysis, audit, attestations, and agent trust.
- **Mirror:** self-checkpointing operational recovery and reproducible state.
- **GhostPay:** programmable payment streaming.
- **RefiRail:** one-action position repair/refinance with a before/after financial receipt.
- **CyberPuck Chaos:** game-first AI intervention.
- **Alter Ego:** private wallet-history analysis and behavioral policy.

The common catalog primitive—private inputs producing a signed minimum-disclosure result—is not differentiation by itself. A survivor must be distinguished by the asset edge state, the unique authorization it produces, and the completed user outcome.

## Semantic Deduplication Groups

### G1. Interrupted cross-chain intent correction

**Members:** `LATE ROUTE`, `TAGBACK`  
**Shared semantic core:** FDC-proven XRP payment does not match the intended Flare/FAsset consequence; private claim/intent data produces one redirect, delivery, quarantine, or refund authorization.  
**Strongest representative:** **TAGBACK × LATE ROUTE synthesis** — retain TAGBACK's two-sided private claim matcher and nullifier, but use LATE ROUTE's explicit mint/quarantine/final-disposition lifecycle so the corrected value is not an unexplained reserve fixture.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on external-fact trigger + privacy-preserving value flow; adjacency to GhostPay only at the payment domain.  
**Dami repeat check:** no shipped or in-flight project completes wrong-reference/mismatched-intent recovery across XRPL and FXRP. This is clear if the cross-chain exception—not generic payments—is kept central.

### G2. Confidential commercial entitlement and partial settlement

**Members:** `VEILED CHANGE ORDER`, `VEILCHECK ESCROW`  
**Shared semantic core:** private contract/work evidence is evaluated into a full, partial, abstain, or refund result that releases FXRP.  
**Strongest representative:** **VEILCHECK ESCROW**, with VEILED CHANGE ORDER's policy expiry and challenge-locked remainder added only if they remain small. Hidden acceptance tests create a more deterministic demo than natural-language scope adjudication.  
**Catalog relation:** strong convergence on verifiable private decision + privacy-preserving value flow.  
**Dami repeat check:** substantial adjacency/overlap with Verdikt settlement adjudication and solv-001 paid-agent work. It is not an in-flight duplicate on current evidence, but it repeats a familiar Dami product shape and should remain outside the recommended synthesis pool unless the acceptance-test inclusion proof becomes the unmistakable product.

### G3. Confidential signer continuity with completed external payment

**Members:** `PAYROLL PULSE`, `MACHINE LIFEBOAT`  
**Shared semantic core:** a confidential machine disappears or rotates; the successor reconciles private policy and pending intent before PMW performs one bounded XRPL action and FDC closes it.  
**Strongest representative:** **PAYROLL PULSE × MACHINE LIFEBOAT synthesis** — PAYROLL PULSE supplies a named employer and visible three-recipient outcome; MACHINE LIFEBOAT contributes signer epochs, stale-intent refusal, and one-action rotation permits.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on policy-controlled execution + local safety boundary + live receipt.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacent to Backstop and AgentTreasury, and close to EdgeLedger's hash-bound signer/abstention/reconciliation path. It remains distinct only because machine failover is the trigger and an owed external payroll batch is the completed recovery. A generic treasury or generic co-signer framing would be a repeat.

### G4. FAsset exception diagnosis that completes repair

**Members:** `REDEMPTION REWIND`, `REPAIR RECEIPT`  
**Shared semantic core:** FDC establishes a stalled/partial asset lifecycle; FCC privately selects a bounded retry, reroute, challenge, or return; a before/after receipt proves restored value.  
**Strongest representative:** **REDEMPTION REWIND**, enriched with REPAIR RECEIPT's deterministic evidence capsule and stale-proof rejection. Its user and economic state are clearer than a generalized operator repair tool.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on closed-loop diagnosis/action, external-fact trigger, and honest evidence labels.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacent to Mirror's recovery evidence and RefiRail's before/after repair, but neither prior project resolves an FDC-confirmed FAsset redemption exception through confidential counterfactual authorization. Clear if it stays one redemption case rather than becoming incident infrastructure.

### G5. Individual private unwind or deleveraging

**Members:** `COLLATERAL CIRCUIT BREAKER`, `QUIET EXIT WINDOW`, `EXIT ENVELOPE`  
**Shared semantic core:** hidden exposure/loss bounds are privately simulated into a one-use FXRP unwind, repay, or abstention permit.  
**Strongest representative:** **COLLATERAL CIRCUIT BREAKER** for the smallest-safe-action mechanic and explicit all-branches-unsafe abstention. QUIET EXIT WINDOW has the cleanest Smart Account envelope, while EXIT ENVELOPE is the simplest, but neither adds a substantially different product loop.  
**Catalog relation:** heavy convergence on private DeFi state machines, guardians, and closed-loop diagnosis.  
**Dami repeat check:** high overlap with GhostFund's private yield vault, adjacency to ShadowDesk+/Axon confidential execution, and adjacency to RefiRail's position repair. Do not advance these standalone; use only their smallest-safe-action and abstention mechanics as ingredients in a more differentiated concept.

### G6. Scarce confidential exit allocation

**Member:** `VEILED EXIT`  
**Distinct semantic core:** multiple hidden exit requests compete for FDC-confirmed limited redemption capacity; FCC proves inclusion and computes fair fills/refunds.  
**Strongest representative:** **VEILED EXIT**.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on privacy-preserving value flow, novel DeFi state machine, and first-session countdown; the proportional capacity allocator is the novel mechanic.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacent to GhostFund/ShadowDesk+ because it hides exit intent, but not a repeat: the product is scarce-capacity allocation with omission protection and deterministic remainder refunds, not yield management or private trading.

### G7. Confidential credit correction and appeal

**Member:** `SECOND LOOK CREDIT`  
**Distinct semantic core:** corrected private evidence and policy-version comparison produce a one-use collateral amendment.  
**Strongest representative:** **SECOND LOOK CREDIT**.  
**Catalog relation:** direct convergence on confidential credit plus forced authorization expiry.  
**Dami repeat check:** overlap with shipped Veil and 9ncore, plus an in-flight product-category conflict with PrivLend. The appeal state is novel, but the product remains confidential lending/credit. Exclude from synthesis under the in-flight duplicate rule.

### G8. Reversible confidential beneficiary recovery

**Member:** `KINSHIP WINDOW`  
**Distinct semantic core:** external inactivity opens, and renewed activity cancels, a challengeable recovery window; FCC reveals only beneficiary eligibility before FXRP release.  
**Strongest representative:** **KINSHIP WINDOW**.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on external-fact trigger + private decision + countdown, with a novel reversible-handoff state machine.  
**Dami repeat check:** no material shipped or in-flight repeat. It is adjacent to policy recovery generally, but the beneficiary commitment, owner-return cancellation, and final continuity transfer form a new causal loop.

### G9. Confidential security work followed by payout

**Members:** `PROOFHUSH BOUNTY`, `FIXBOND`  
**Shared semantic core:** hidden security evidence or patch is privately reproduced; a signed success/severity result repairs or releases FXRP compensation.  
**Strongest representative:** **FIXBOND** for the stronger proof-of-fix and user-value restoration; PROOFHUSH BOUNTY has the clearer protected-disclosure story but ends at a familiar audit/bounty verdict.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on stake-backed claims, private evaluation, lineage, and live performance receipts.  
**Dami repeat check:** substantial overlap with AgentMesh and 0g-sentinel security analysis, AgentAuditor's trust/audit direction, and solv-001 paid work. FIXBOND adds an asset repair, but the combined audit-for-payment surface is still a strong portfolio repeat. Exclude from the synthesis pool.

### G10. Forced authorization lifecycle and split-policy resolution

**Members:** `MANDATE ZERO`, `SPLITLOCK`  
**Shared semantic core:** confidential policy is not permanently valid; authority either decays and is recomputed or conflicting versions collapse to a common safe intersection/abstention.  
**Strongest representative:** **MANDATE ZERO × SPLITLOCK synthesis** — use deterministic zero-expiry as the primary mechanic and allow renewal only from the safe intersection of the old commitment and current private policy.  
**Catalog relation:** strong convergence on policy-controlled execution and forced memory scarcity; policy decay is the differentiating mechanic.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacent to Backstop and AgentTreasury and close to EdgeLedger's versioned, hash-bound signer authorization. A generic transaction guardian is a repeat. It remains viable only as a deliberately self-erasing Smart Account mandate whose inability to act is visible and economically enforced.

### G11. Private merchant claim to external refund

**Member:** `CIPHER REFUND RELAY`  
**Distinct semantic core:** FDC proves the original payment; FCC privately adjudicates invoice/claim/fraud evidence; PMW sends the exact external XRP refund with nullifier protection.  
**Strongest representative:** **CIPHER REFUND RELAY**.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on external-fact trigger + policy-controlled action + privacy-preserving value flow.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacency to GhostPay at the payments layer and to Verdikt at confidential adjudication, but no prior project closes a private merchant claim as a least-authority XRPL refund. Clear if refund execution and duplicate refusal remain the hero flow.

### G12. Private event inclusion with deterministic failure refund

**Member:** `VOID RECEIPT`  
**Distinct semantic core:** FCC proves all hidden positions were included and FDC supplies resolved/cancelled/stale status; the contract must pay or refund every locked FXRP stake.  
**Strongest representative:** **VOID RECEIPT**.  
**Catalog relation:** convergence on private outcome resolution, external-fact triggers, and countdown demos; omission challenge plus mandatory refund is the differentiator.  
**Dami repeat check:** adjacent to in-flight EdgeLedger because both use external event truth, but not a semantic duplicate: EdgeLedger is an autonomous forecasting/trading system, while VOID RECEIPT is participant inclusion and asset-safe round cancellation. It is also merely adjacent to CyberPuck's game surface. Keep the product framed as settlement integrity, not prediction alpha or gaming.

## Recommended Unscored Synthesis Pool

Advance these eight concepts to feasibility, collision, and later scoring gates. Their order is not a ranking.

1. **TAGBACK × LATE ROUTE — Cross-Chain Intent Rescue**  
   FDC-proven mismatched/late XRP payment → private two-sided claim match → real FXRP quarantine → one redirect, delivery, or refund → nullified reconciliation receipt.

2. **REDEMPTION REWIND × REPAIR RECEIPT — Bounded FAsset Recovery**  
   Partial/stalled redemption → deterministic evidence capsule → private counterfactual branch choice → one capped retry/challenge/return → before/after asset proof.

3. **PAYROLL PULSE × MACHINE LIFEBOAT — Confidential Payroll Failover**  
   Primary machine loss → signer-epoch and stale-intent checks → one bounded PMW payroll batch → FDC reconciliation and replay refusal.

4. **KINSHIP WINDOW — Reversible Asset Continuity**  
   External inactivity → private beneficiary eligibility → public challenge window → owner-activity cancellation or final FXRP release.

5. **VEILED EXIT — Scarce Redemption Allocator**  
   Hidden exit commitments → FDC-confirmed capacity → private inclusion/fair allocation → FXRP redemption and deterministic remainder refunds.

6. **MANDATE ZERO × SPLITLOCK — Self-Erasing Asset Authority**  
   Smart Account authority decays to zero → current and prior private policies resolve to a safe intersection → one-cycle authorization or signed abstention.

7. **CIPHER REFUND RELAY — Confidential External Refund**  
   FDC-proven purchase → private claim adjudication → least-authority PMW refund → duplicate/timeout refusal and external settlement receipt.

8. **VOID RECEIPT — Inclusion-Proven Failure Settlement**  
   Hidden round commitments → FDC event status → private inclusion audit → mandatory FXRP payout or cancellation refund with omission challenge.

## Synthesis Guardrails

- Do not merge all recovery ideas into a platform. Each candidate must retain one named user, one edge state, and one 90-second consequence.
- Do not present FCC/FCE signing as novelty. The differentiator must be the state machine it uniquely authorizes.
- Keep exactly one interoperable primitive primary per candidate; supporting FDC evidence does not excuse a passive asset leg.
- Preserve independent removal tests after synthesis. Adding a sibling's safeguard must not turn either track into optional infrastructure.
- Treat `SECOND LOOK CREDIT`, `PROOFHUSH BOUNTY`, and `FIXBOND` as collision-excluded, not merely lower-priority.
- Do not revive G5 as a generic private vault, portfolio guardian, or trading terminal; its reusable contribution is only smallest-safe-action plus abstention.
- Before scoring, verify live/testnet feasibility for each primary FAssets, Smart Account, PMW, and FDC action and downgrade any fixture-dependent asset consequence.
