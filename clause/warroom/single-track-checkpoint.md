# Flare Summer Signal — Single-Track Checkpoint

## Result

**No finalist.** Rounds 5 and 6 each generated 20 blind, schema-valid Interoperable Asset concepts. The complete hard-gate audits found **0 survivors out of 40**.

## What failed

The concepts usually contained one real Flare action, but the proposed product around it did not hold up end to end:

- some depended on an unnamed vault, lending market, router, signer, or application interface;
- some assumed authority or asset outputs the native protocol does not provide;
- some duplicated native redemption, liquidation, challenge, recovery, or Smart Account behavior;
- the remainder reduced to familiar take-profit, stop-loss, threshold harvest, reserve, keeper-bounty, guard, receipt, or reward-split mechanics;
- several hero demos required an uncontrollable liquidation, challenge, FDC proof, epoch, or external actor.

The gates were not weakened, and killed ideas were not scored or repaired after freeze. There is no honest “best rejected idea” to promote.

## Decision needed

Choose one:

1. **Recommended — restart from one named real user and observed workflow.** Secure access to a holder, agent, vault operator, liquidator, or application team; document the exact recurring failure, authority, current workaround, and callable interfaces; then generate narrowly around that evidence.
2. **End Warroom with no finalist.** Preserve the evidence and submit nothing from this search.
3. **Explicitly change one constraint.** Name the gate or scope change and why accepting the resulting market, novelty, integration, or demo risk is worth it.
