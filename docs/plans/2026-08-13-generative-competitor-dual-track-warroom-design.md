# Generative Competitor and Dual-Track Warroom Design

## Objective

Correct Warroom so the complete competitor corpus drives discovery before it filters collisions, and require every Flare Summer Signal finalist to contain deep, load-bearing integrations for both official tracks.

## Root cause

The previous Warroom input stack gave every generator a kill list but assigned the competitor landscape to only one generator. This structurally encouraged workflow-first ideation followed by defensive competitor checking. It could not reliably discover missing outcomes or portable mechanisms across the 99-signal corpus.

## Selected design

Warroom setup builds two shared generative artifacts before dispatch:

1. The existing Working Primitives Sheet derived from past winners.
2. A Competitor Opportunity Map derived from the complete current field.

Every generator receives both generative artifacts. Generators use different lenses, but none operate without the competitor map. Each idea must cite a corpus-derived strength or portable mechanism and a missing outcome, underserved job, or edge state. Named collisions, saturated surfaces, Dami's prior and in-flight projects, differentiation prompts, and the kill list remain in gate-only appendices withheld until the raw idea and reasoning are durably recorded. Unique research slices are sanitized against the same gate-only material before dispatch.

## Flare track strategy

The active brief declares `multi-track required`.

- Interoperable Asset Products must contribute a real asset lifecycle through FAssets, FXRP, Smart Accounts, PMW external execution, or FDC-verified asset state.
- Confidential Compute Apps must contribute a necessary FCC or FCE operation with an explicit trust boundary and verifiable result.
- Each integration gets an independent removal test.
- Every finalist needs one causal proof path joining the asset action and confidential operation.
- Two unrelated features, passive reads, token balances, ordinary encryption, and unverified TEE claims fail.

## Pipeline changes

- Setup mines the complete competitor corpus into a structured opportunity map.
- Generation adds competitor-derived insight, missing outcome, multi-track architecture, per-track removal tests, and joined proof path to every idea.
- Gating rejects ideas whose corpus appears only in differentiation or whose track integrations are cosmetic.
- Scoring reports competitor leverage and each required track's depth independently.
- Fact checking verifies corpus provenance and exact per-track technical claims.
- `WINNER-BRIEF.md` carries the complete generative lineage and dual-track proof contract into Forge.

## Verification

The executable contract test at `.codex/skills/hackathon-warroom/tests/generative-competitor-contract.sh` must fail against the previous skill behavior and pass after the correction. The Flare active brief and PULSE must contain the same multi-track and corpus-first requirements.

## Boundary

This design does not generate or select product ideas. Warroom runs in a clean chat after handoff.
