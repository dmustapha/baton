## BLOCKER-Level Concerns (must be resolved / de-risked before or at build Phase 0)

**BLOCKER-1 — The entire thesis is one un-run assumption, and its fallback is a losing product.**
Task-0 (live operator executes our custom `Call[]`) is un-run. The plan correctly gates on it at hour ~2. But the documented fallback — FDC-Payment single-asset checkout — is a *plain FXRP deposit*, which is exactly the saturated "simple FXRP utility" the brief kills. If Task-0 fails, Baton doesn't just lose the differentiator; it becomes a bottom-quartile entry in the most crowded cluster.
*Mitigation already present:* fail-fast at hr 2 leaves ~20h to pivot. *What's missing:* a **better** fallback than plain checkout. See E-5 (a single-vault-but-still-Smart-Accounts-`Call[]` fallback preserves the "impossible without Flare" story even if multi-vault composition fails). This is the highest-leverage pre-build fix.

**BLOCKER-2 — Atomicity is the thesis but is not made judge-visible.**
The differentiator (F-001/F-003) is "one atomic tx, two deposits, all-or-nothing." The demo shows two balances going up and narrates atomicity. A skeptical judge cannot distinguish "one atomic multi-call" from "two sequential deposits" without being shown the internal calls of a single tx hash. Scene 4 must explicitly land on the explorer's internal-transactions / decoded-input view of ONE `executeUserOp` containing both deposits, on screen, with a callout. Without this, the escape from "simple FXRP vault" is asserted, not proven. (E-3)
